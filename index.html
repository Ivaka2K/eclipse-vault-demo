ECLIPSE VAULT · GAME INFORMATION

Paytable and rules

Game configuration

5 reels × 3 rows · 20 fixed paylines. Payouts are paid left-to-right. Line bet × 20 = total stake.

Wild and Scatter

Wild Solar Crown substitutes all regular symbols but not Scatter. Scatter Eclipse Gate pays no line award and triggers Free Spins anywhere on the grid.

Free Spins

3 / 4 / 5 Scatter award 8 / 12 / 16 Free Spins. A Wild increases the multiplier for the next free spin, up to 5×. 3+ Scatter retrigger +4 spins; awarded spins cap: 30.

Buy Bonus demo option

Buy Bonus costs 40× total stake in demo credits, shows exactly 3 Scatter and starts 8 Free Spins. It is a prototype-only assumption; a commercial version requires separate RTP and compliance validation.

Paytable per 1.00 line bet

This local demo executes the same visible reel-strip logic as the coursework model. Full RTP remains a simulated coursework estimate, not a certified value.
